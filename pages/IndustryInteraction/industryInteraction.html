Industry Interaction

Industry Institute Coordination Cell (IICC)

  • The Industry Institute Coordination Cell (IICC) of the college was established in 2008 with the aim to bridge the gap between the Institute and the Industry. We organize annual Industry Institute Research Meets to showcase institutional research and establish collaborations with industries.
  • Our students regularly participate in industrial visits related to their courses of study. These visits help them understand work culture, technological advancements, and industry expectations. During the pandemic, we successfully conducted virtual industrial visits.
  • The IICC facilitates consultancy works through departmental expertise and maintains an industry-expert mapping database. A key function of our cell is providing mandatory internships for students to gain academic credits, with industry experts contributing to curriculum development through Boards of Studies.
  • We collaborate with the placement cell to connect students with valuable internship opportunities. Regular interactions with industry experts through guest lectures and workshops in advanced technologies form a crucial part of our activities. We particularly value contributions from our alumni working in industries, who guide current students through special sessions.

Industry Internship Program

Internships provide practical exposure to the industry which is required for an engineering student. It forms an important part of the engineering study. Students should utilize the period of internship to observe and learn from the working places in the industry.
